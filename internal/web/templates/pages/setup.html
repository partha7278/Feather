First-time setup

Create the administrator account

It signs in to this panel and decides what gets archived — and whether it is then deleted.

At least {{.Data.MinPassword}} characters. This account can schedule deletions from production, so use a passphrase you use nowhere else.

Keep the master key with your backups Credentials you add later are encrypted with a key stored outside the database. Without it they cannot be read. You can download it any time from Settings → Storage. To move Feather to another server, use Settings → Export & import — the bundle is sealed with a passphrase, not this key.
