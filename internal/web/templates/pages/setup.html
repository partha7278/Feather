Create the administrator account

This is the first and only step before feather is usable. The account you create here signs in to this panel, where you add MongoDB accounts, backup destinations, and the retention rules that decide what gets archived — and whether it is then deleted.

At least {{.Data.MinPassword}} characters. This account can schedule deletions from your production databases, so use a passphrase you do not use anywhere else.

Credentials you enter later are encrypted with a master key stored outside the database. Keep a copy of that key file with your backups — without it the stored credentials cannot be read.
